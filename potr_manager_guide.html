Introduction

Welcome to POTR Payroll Pro! This app handles all calculations, penalties, night multipliers, and allowances based on the POTR operations manual rules. No manual calculations are required. Follow these simple parts to run payroll every week.

Part 1: Setting up the Week and Sales

When you open the app on Monday night or Tuesday morning:

  1. Select the Week Ending Date: Use the date selector in the top right corner to select the Monday that ends your pay cycle.
  2. Enter Sales Metrics: In the Dashboard tab, input:
    • Weekly Total Receipts ($)
    • True Sales ($)
    • Guest Count
💡 Pro-Tip
The app automatically calculates your wage percentage relative to sales. If the bar glows green, your labor cost matches our target budget!

Part 2: Entering Hours Worked (Timecards)

Go to the Timecards tab and select an employee's name from the sidebar. You can record their hours in two ways:

Option A: The AI Camera Scanner (Recommended)

  1. Click the blue Scan Handwritten Card button.
  2. If using a phone/iPad, tap Capture Photo and snap a clear picture of their handwritten card. If you already took a photo, click Upload File and select it.
  3. Click Start OCR Scan. The AI will extract their shifts.
  4. Review the extracted times. If any box needs correction, click it and type the correct time.
  5. Click Apply Scanned Data to save it.

Option B: Typing Hours by Hand

  1. Click the toggle switch under the Status column to mark a day as "Worked" (turns gold).
  2. Enter the Start Time (e.g. 09:00) and End Time (e.g. 17:00).
  3. Enter the Break in minutes (e.g. 30).
  4. Tick checkboxes if they worked as a Supervisor, a Split Shift, or on a Public Holiday.

Part 3: The Roster Check (Compliance Audit)

Go to the Roster tab to verify scheduled hours versus actual hours worked:

  1. Type in the scheduled hours from your roster next to each employee.
  2. Look at the variance:
    • Red Alert (e.g. +4.00): Employee worked overtime.
    • Orange Alert (e.g. -3.00): Employee finished early or started late.
  3. If the variance is more than 2 hours: You must enter a brief explanation in the Manager Variance Reason box (e.g. "Kitchen busy due to local sports event").
⚠️ Mandatory Rule
The app will block you from locking payroll for the week if any variance greater than 2 hours is left without an explanatory comment.

Part 4: Locking and Exporting

  1. Return to the Dashboard tab and click Finalize & Lock Week. This locks calculations and updates leave balances.
  2. Go to the Payroll Sheets tab to extract reports:
    • Print Sheet: Formats costing/calculation tables on clean pages.
    • Xero Export / MYOB Export: Downloads timesheets ready to import.
    • Download ABA Bank File: Downloads bank instructions to pay everyone in one click.
    • Email to Admin: Opens a pre-filled draft email to Head Office.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The scanner camera won't open on my tablet/phone.
Make sure camera permissions are enabled for the browser. If it still won't load, use your device's normal camera app to take a photo first, then use the "Upload File" option in the app.
The OCR scanner misread a handwritten number. How do I fix it?
You can easily edit any field. Simply click on the misread time or break duration in the grid and type the correct number.
Can casual employees be paid sick or annual leave?
No. Under the Guest Assistant award manual, casuals are not entitled to paid leave. The calculation engine automatically caps casual leave overrides to 0.
Where is the data stored? Will I lose it if I close the tab?
No. All data is saved automatically in your device's local browser memory. You can close the tab or reboot the device, and your entries will be restored.
